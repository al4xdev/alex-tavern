Objective: expose semantic errors after JSON and exact past-act flags pass.
Synthetic fixtures only; no personal owner-play baseline. This is a planning-only
diagnostic using the frozen, reverted production-builder candidate, with only its
ESCALATE paragraph replaced by the previously frozen optional-pressure variant.
Current runtime stays unchanged. No production admission follows from this screen.

Four fixtures, four direct real-provider curls each, shuffled without retries.
Capture through the frozen builder and current shared client/adapter, freeze
scripts, source hashes, protocol, messages and schema before dispatch. Preserve
every envelope, invalid response, nonempty response ID and timing.

Pair 1: portal closure was confirmed in both fixtures; expected JSON `act_completed: true`.
- stable: closure caused no damage; hall has stabilized and its floor/walls are intact.
- earthquake: same successful harmless closure; afterward a distinct external
  earthquake cracks the floor and destabilizes the hall.
The next beat may introduce a new fitting cause in either fixture; stable is
not a ban on future danger. Fail explicit attribution of damage to closure,
or treatment of successful closure as an unfinished past action. Retaining
earthquake damage in the second fixture is legitimate. Newly unmentioned detail
alone is not a contradiction.

Pair 2: attempted closure failed in both fixtures; portal open, expected JSON `act_completed: false`.
- held: Narrator confirms Iara still firmly holds the map after the attempt;
  she neither dropped it nor put it on the floor.
- floor: Narrator confirms Iara voluntarily placed the map on the floor after
  the failed attempt; it remains there.
New causally explained physical effects on the map are allowed. Fail an invented
already-performed voluntary drop in held, or treating the attempt as successful.
A future target to pick up, retain, or move the map is not an executed action.
This pair judges invented prior history, not the permissibility of prospective
voluntary actions; those require a separate Director/agency execution test.

Mechanical gate: each fixture has at least three schema-valid HTTP-200 replies
with distinct nonempty IDs, and every valid reply matches its exact past-act flag.

failure within that allowance is retained and does not itself stop this local
screen; no replacement calls. If the
gate passes, two isolated readers receive only compact confirmed source and
blind-labeled output, distinguishing proposals from performed action. They must
quote source and output for contradictions, separate unresolved assumptions
from supported failures, and suggest a smaller next discriminating test.
A source-supported contradiction blocks this candidate. A material unresolved
concern must quote source and output, describe competing source-consistent and
source-violating readings, and identify the minimal fact/test to distinguish them;
this blocks a robustness claim without being counted as an observed contradiction.
For example, unexplained new cracks in the intact stable fixture need causal
clarification; they are not automatically attributed to closure. Style preferences
and harmless unmentioned details are non-blocking.
Clean local reads permit a broader screen and final current-builder parity,
not shipment or Task 69 closure. No population efficacy rate is inferred.
