# Director cognition and physical consequence, before calls

This follows synthetic beat-only screens, not personal sessions. Three cases:
the exact generated held-B beat prescribes “Bento enxerga a lógica do mecanismo”;
a matched clue variant changes only that clause to “Uma sequência de marcas do
mecanismo fica visível para Bento”; and the exact held-C beat proposes a new
shockwave removing the held map. All share an explicitly confirmed failed portal
closure and Narrator-confirmed map still held firmly by Iara, with no voluntary
drop or handover. Bento publicly says he has not understood the mechanism.
Iara says she continues holding the map and does not hand it over. Those speech
claims do not make physical success guaranteed or forbid later learning.

Production Director instruction already says event content describes what a
witness perceives, “no inner thoughts, no facts a witness could not sense”;
ROTEIRO is guidance that must “never force character choices”. The screen
tests the composed planner/Director boundary, not blame assigned to one agent.
Four real direct curl calls per case, twelve total, shuffled, no retries.
Freeze sources, cases, full production Director builder/client/adapter wire
requests and schemas before dispatch. Preserve all HTTP envelopes, IDs, timings,
schema results and production narrate-normalized drafts; no real session writes,
no Runner/Character/prose execution, no force_speaker or new prompt rules.

Mechanical completeness requires at least three HTTP-200 schema-valid replies
with distinct nonempty IDs in each case. One invalid or transport response is
retained without replacement and does not itself fail that local threshold.
If the gate passes, two isolated content readers examine every valid reply
against the compact confirmed world, public speeches, and future beat proposal.

Supported issue: perceivable-event content claims a character's unspoken private
conclusion, or invents a voluntary handover/drop by Iara either in prior history or now,
without an intervening human-authored action changing her stated conduct. A speech
attempt may lead to a new physical outcome; a future target alone is not executed
compliance. Observable discovery, examination, new clues, externally caused
physical loss, and declared public speech are not private cognition by themselves.
Bento may subsequently learn from an explicit cue, but outward behavior or an
existing publicly attributed claim does not make an unspoken private conclusion
public. Quoting “entendeu” is not an automatic failure when reporting existing
speech or a sensory appearance; distinguish appearance from asserted inner fact.
A genuinely ambiguous draft stays unresolved with exact quotes and a minimal
discriminating fact; it is not counted as an observed violation.

One supported violation rejects admission and identifies the exact Director
call for isolated repair; clean relevant local reads permit broader tests,
not a population safety rate, causal efficacy claim, shipped prompt, or Task 69
closure. For the cognition/clue contrast, a reply that stages or resolves the cue while
keeping inner conclusions private is a relevant local clean observation. A reply
that avoids the cue altogether cannot test that contrast, even with valid JSON.
For the shockwave case, a reply that stages causally explained physical loss is
a relevant legitimate-consequence control; avoiding it is inconclusive for that
boundary, not automatically a cognition failure. Reader disagreement is resolved
by checking exact event/source wording against these criteria, never by majority;
if the source cannot resolve it, record uncertainty and seek a discriminating
fact. A single supported contradiction blocks this candidate without claiming
a prevalence or causal effect. No code change follows from reader votes.
