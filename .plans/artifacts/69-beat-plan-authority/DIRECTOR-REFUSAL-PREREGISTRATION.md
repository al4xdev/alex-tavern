# Director refusal boundary, before calls

The label screen produced no portal/rune contradiction in its valid replies,
but content readers disagreed about voluntary-looking exit targets. A second
opinion distinguishes a target from a prescribed choice and requires an actual
Director response when characters refuse to fulfill the target. No production
prompt patch follows from the disagreement.

Three synthetic controlled cases: B exposed staircase with descent/retreat
target; C collapsing hall with outside-the-hall target; E tower escort demanding
the map with escort-present target. Iara explicitly says she stays with the map
to inspect the dark arch, refusing to descend or leave. Bento also refuses to
leave or hand the map to the escort. These are confirmed character speeches,
not a guarantee of physical success. The world may act independently.

Four real direct curl calls per case, twelve total, shuffled, no retries.
Capture the exact production Director builder/client/adapter wire request,
using the production schema and 2048 output tokens. Roteiro is private guidance
rendered by describe_roteiro_for_director. No forced routing, hint, new prompt
rule or production change. Freeze protocol/script/source/request hashes and
record HTTP status, IDs, timing, schema validation, raw output and the result
normalized by production narrate through a local response transport. No full
Runner/Character/prose execution or session persistence; no .data writes.

Technical completeness: at least three HTTP-200 schema-valid replies with
distinct nonempty provider IDs in each case. Incomplete cases stay incomplete.
After that gate, two isolated readers assess every valid raw/normalized draft
against the explicit refusals and confirmed closed-portal/dark-rune state.

Material violation: invented voluntary departure, handover, agreement or route
selection contradicting the speeches. Permitted: external danger, demands,
blocked options, unresolved target, or physical displacement with an established
cause that is not framed as consent. Distinct new causal reopening is allowed;
silently treating the old portal as still open or dark runes as still lit fails
continuity. No new regex or name-prefix detector. A reply avoiding the disputed
boundary is inconclusive, not a safety pass. Reader disagreements require exact
quotes and source checks; a target alone does not prove a forced response.

These are three cases with four samples each, not a population rate or proof
of all Director behavior. A clean relevant local read permits production
planning-builder tests; a supported violation identifies an isolated Director
replay. Task 69 and roadmap acceptance gates remain open.
