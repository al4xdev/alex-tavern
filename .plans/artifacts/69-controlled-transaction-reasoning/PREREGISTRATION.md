# Controlled physical transaction screen, before calls

Task69's durable store has no admitted model producer. The September ordered
transaction screen used thinking disabled and failed source/operation fidelity.
That historical result remains failed; no owner-play/archive payload is used as
a baseline here. Reasoning could affect this contract, but improvement is
unproved. This experiment tests a fresh producer candidate, not runtime delivery.

Five controlled two-portal fixtures share public cast Iara/Bento/Téo and explicit
canonical starting apertures/positions, declared attempts and a proposed draft:
1. Blue closed/barred, ineffective Téo push: reject proposed fresh closure/
crossing, retain Bento's warning and Iara's lantern support.
2. Blue ajar, sufficient external impact: retain legal opening, wounded guard
entry/collapse, Bento's reported order and Iara's shield support.
3. Blue closed, Iara/Bento already beyond: block Téo's ineffective push; no
re-crossing those already beyond, keep Iara's backpack adjustment.
4. Blue closed and green ajar: green opens after sufficient wind impact while
Téo's blue attempt stays blocked; preserve different portal identities.
5. Blue previously closed, new Bento unlatching/pulling intervention: allow
fresh blue opening and Téo's declared crossing; retain Iara's lamp in the tunnel.

Each fixture asks for one ordered transaction containing an ARRAY of steps.
Each step has kind aperture_change, attempt_result or other; the transaction
may combine any number of those steps. Every field has a local description and
irrelevant fields are empty. An ajar gap in these fixtures is too narrow for a
person to cross until it opens; this condition is also sent in the model input.
Aperture changes and tracked cast crossings must use typed steps; other cannot
hide them. Fixture2's wounded guard is not a registered cast member; other may
describe his entry/collapse after the typed opening, but cannot hide a crossing
by Iara, Bento or Téo. This explicitly leaves unregistered entities outside the
mechanical crossing boundary. Other retains consequential non-aperture actions and reported speech
intents without authoring dialogue. Target/actor enums include all public labels
and the entire roster, with no named human/operator or singled-out enum.
No natural-language gate/crossing sentence is assembled by a grammar template;
read the actual transaction, not an ungrammatical harness paraphrase.

Two arms differ ONLY in thinking wire settings: disabled versus enabled/high,
same model/current shared adapter, schema/messages/max_tokens8192. Four fresh
logical calls per fixture/arm, forty calls. Actual chat_completion_json runs
over curl transport with its existing maximum three attempts. All raw requests,
envelopes, validation errors/retries, terminal results, reasoning presence and
IDs retained in isolated /tmp/debug and ignored artifacts. No replacement
logical calls or semantic-error repair calls. Freeze schema/source/script/
requests/protocol before dispatch. Both arms undergo the same mechanical and
blinded text evaluation. Low thinking is diagnostic, not a production setting.

A mechanical validator resolves exact public labels to seeded durable entities,
checks kind-specific field shape, applies aperture edges via the real store and
checks tracked crossings against current aperture/position and declared attempts.
This fixture declares an ajar gap too narrow for crossing. Mechanical checks
cannot detect physical claims hidden in other/cause or material-event loss;
the independent Gemini text reader receives source plus whole transactions, opaque shuffled
labels, and must quote such violations and any unsupported cause or actor
choice. The main agent checks quoted objections against literal source/output.
Unresolved objections receive a second isolated text reading of existing
material; a remaining material ambiguity stops the candidate. No vote or
new generation resolves a dispute.

Local candidate rule: all twenty high-thinking logical calls must terminate
schema-valid and mechanically legal; every high output must meet its fixture's
physical/whole-event obligations and no hidden tracked transition/crossing.
Any high failure stops this exact candidate. A low failure cannot license a high
failure. If both arms pass all content/operation obligations, no reasoning gain
was observed here. If high passes and disabled fails, report the per-fixture
sample difference as a diagnostic observation, not reliable superiority or an
explanation of the September failure. Low-arm findings therefore alter the
interpretation of the screen, without changing high's acceptance requirements.
Report per-fixture first-attempt/final schema acceptance and mechanical/
content findings, not population error rates or a causal account of past failures.
A pass is only a local producer signal: no runtime admission, dynamic registration,
other physical dimensions, renderer fidelity, per-viewer containment, consecutive
Runner submissions or Task69 closure is established. The free other boundary
remains an architectural risk even if these finite samples contain no bypass.
