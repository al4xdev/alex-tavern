# Controlled beat-only baseline, preregistered before calls

Purpose: inspect current planning coherence in synthetic canonical states, excluding all personal owner-play sessions. This is a diagnostic of production builders and deterministic triggers, not a candidate patch or a complete Task 69 acceptance test.

Nine fixtures, frozen before calls:
1. Abandoned room: both characters have already left a museum vault by transport and stand at a distant canyon camp. Old beat asks pressure at the vault's service door. No established route back. Reject treating the vault door/guards as locally present without a newly planned, causally specified return or connection.
2. Broken bridge: the bridge has already fallen; both characters remain on the near bank. Old act expected keeping the crossing open for a courier. Reject another destruction of that same intact bridge, or crossing its remains as though passable without a new repair/route.
3. Within-room move: characters move from a window to the opposite side of the same council room. Their archive-admission negotiation remains unsettled. A new pressure is allowed; the reposition itself does not meet the existing act's exit condition or establish travel to another scene.
4. Continuing journey: the pair has arrived at an intermediate watchtower, with a warning still undelivered at the mountain observatory. The journey's objective remains relevant. Reject claiming delivery completed or automatically discarding the warning because the location changed.
5. Completed escape: both have already escaped a flooding locked chamber to the terrace. The current act's exit condition is that both are outside. The next act concerns getting a signal lens to a tower. Require act_completed=true and no renewed first escape from the chamber; a future explicit return is a new event, not a completed antecedent.
6. Split party: one character stays at the riverbank, the other is already at the isolated watchtower; the initial raft-launch act remains unfinished. Reject treating them as co-present without a planned transfer or communication path, and reject deciding either character's response.

7. Portal attempt: an attempted closure explicitly failed; portal remains open. Require act_completed=false.
8. Portal closed: Narrator confirmed complete closure and extinguished runes; both remain in the hall. Require act_completed=true.
9. Portal left: both crossed to the canyon, then portal closed and runes extinguished; no return passage. Require act_completed=true and no locally present hall/portal unless a new causal connection is planned.

The three portal cases are separately controlled branches, not successive generated turns. This first screen tests the current scheduled planning boundary; a later bounded execution must test attempts over multiple turns and whether replacement beats stop reinstating the resolved problem. A beat change itself does not prove an objective completed.

Each fixture contains the initial act/beat, explicit accepted happenings, current canonical scene, and any attempted character intervention. Two separate observations: first evaluate the deterministic controller after two committed turns at action count two, immediately following the confirmed scene change or control event. Preserve its decision. Then add one short character question as a third committed turn and evaluate at action count three, representing the later ordinary budget/turn-cap boundary. Use the immediate decision if it fired, otherwise the later decision, to build the replacement request. Record which phase was selected. This deliberately separates immediate transition sensitivity from a later scheduled replan; it is not an end-to-end simulation of action counters. Within-room reposition is not expected to satisfy an act exit, but the ordinary cap may still legitimately replan its beat. All nine may therefore generate replacement calls. If neither controller observation returns an action, report that fixture as planning-not-evaluated, not as a pass, and make no calls. Both existing beat and act schemas return act_completed. Use the scope derived from the current decision, without inventing an act-transition builder. Capture the exact wire payload using the current production builder, shared client language/punctuation policy and provider adapter. The current builder has no positions/physical-facts block; do not add one in this baseline. This is a synthetic-input diagnostic using actual builders, not a claim of full production-condition fidelity.

Four real direct curl calls for each triggered fixture, up to 36 total. Secrets go through curl-config stdin; validate configured provider URL before dispatch. Freeze fixture/request/source/protocol hashes; preserve each request, raw response, HTTP status, timing, provider ID and local schema validation. No replacement, prompt tuning, sampling override or regeneration after a failure.

Technical completeness: every triggered fixture needs at least three distinct nonempty provider envelope `id`, HTTP-200 schema-valid replies to support a per-fixture content comparison. A triggered fixture with fewer than three valid replies is marked technically incomplete, not semantically failed; preserve readable replies as individually selected diagnostics only. Other fixtures remain analyzable under the same fixed rule. Failed calls remain visible. No aggregate reliability rate or passing production claim follows from partial or full completion.

Content evaluation: an isolated reader receives the canonical case facts, old act/beat and shuffled anonymized raw replacement plans with character references rendered as public names. No code, tool/controller verdict, hypotheses, expected result or model identity. Ask freely for coherence, impossible antecedents, legitimate developments, compelled character choices and uncertainty; do not ask it to find a preselected defect. Preserve that free read, then have a separate isolated continuity reader check every frozen obligation against each candidate, with quotes. No claimed absence of a failure is based on free reading alone. A material failure means contradiction of an explicit fixture fact, an impossible completed antecedent without a new planned cause, or a compelled character choice. Insufficient spatial/temporal evidence is unresolved and never scored as a pass. Read each plan against its own case, never concatenate alternatives into a narrative.

Decision before results: a source-supported material failure in any valid plan identifies a boundary for a separate isolated follow-up; ambiguous readings are retained as unresolved and do not manufacture a defect. If all valid plans meet the explicit obligations and both reads leave no unresolved material concern, report only local absence of a failure and use independent controlled fixtures next. Unresolved concerns instead yield an inconclusive case and a targeted follow-up. No runtime change or task closure is licensed by this baseline. A beat-only plan may be coherent while its physical execution and prose still fail; Task 69's full acceptance gates remain in force.

Automated first gate: report technical completeness and exact act_completed obligations for the three portal branches, completed escape, within-room move and continuing journey. These explicit facts permit a boolean check; it does not grade plan meaning or eventual execution. A failure stops admission and identifies an isolated replay target. Call content critics only after this gate passes, per the owner's current instruction, to challenge the test and propose adversarial cases. No lexical substring metric is used to declare narrative continuity.
