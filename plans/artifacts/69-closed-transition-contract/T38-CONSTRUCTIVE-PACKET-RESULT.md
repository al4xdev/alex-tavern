The registered **both-text, both-reader gate fails** on S. The author's
`missing_information: null` does not demonstrate that the packet is
sufficient for good S prose: the same author's S text did not pass fiction
review. This one failure does not establish that S cannot be written well
from these facts. The fiction reader explicitly wrote that S lacked
“peso físico ou resposta sensorial ao bloqueio”; the packet does not say
whether Téo touches the metal or stops short. That is a **possible missing
event detail**, not an established requirement to add contact. Adding it
for another test would require a source-grounded, newly registered packet.

manually supplied legal transition. It does not rescue the preceding
stochastic realizer, which made active-opening and temporal-order errors
in L, nor does it resolve S, which failed this fiction review. Task 69
remains open with durable storage but no admitted model producer or

proceed while this boundary remains documented; this result does not
