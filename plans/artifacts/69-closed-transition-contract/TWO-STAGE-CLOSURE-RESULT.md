# Two-stage closure admission: technical pass, Stage A identity failure

The [frozen two-stage screen](TWO-STAGE-CLOSURE-PREREGISTRATION.md) ran seven
independent offered-evidence requests (Stage A) and six independent
full-source retraction requests (Stage B), four fresh curl calls each. All
**52/52** calls returned HTTP 200, distinct provider IDs, schema-valid JSON
and valid literal quotes. No call was replaced. The Stage A synthetic closure
request was run **once** for four repetitions and reused analytically for the
plain, correction and reopening Stage B contexts; it was not counted three
times. The initial unsuffixed artifact paths contained zero-byte files before
preparation and were preserved; this run used the frozen `v2` manifest/directory.

The registered semantic gate **failed at Stage A**. The explicit archived T38
blue-gate closure and the synthetic `O portão azul se fecha com um baque` were
`entailed` in 4/4 calls each. The future warning, static green gate and
wrong-target blue closure were rejected in 4/4 each. But the archived bare T37
fragment `o portão se fecha com um baque surdo`, offered for the **blue** gate,
was incorrectly `entailed` in **2/4** calls; the fragment does not identify the
gate. A longer T37 offer with `fresta do portão azul` followed by the time-skip
`o portão se fecha` was `entailed` in **4/4**, against the frozen negative label.
An independent reader before the calls and a second [content-only read](TWO-STAGE-CLOSURE-CONTENT-PACKET.txt)
afterward both found legitimate referent ambiguity **within the offered
passages**: the offered time-skip summary says the closure seals all groups and
then mentions plural `portões`, so the offered passages do not prove that the
singular closing gate is the blue one. These positive judgments conflict with
the frozen, source-read identity label; they do not establish a general
referent-resolution failure rate or its mechanism.

**Later content-read qualification (2026-09-24):** a separate identity/aspect
screen's independent reader interpreted the longer T37 offer as an unambiguous
anaphor for the blue gate, unlike the two earlier readers. Its frozen negative
label remains the registered decision rule, but the longer offer is now a
**contested literary case**, not established ground truth for a model identity
error. The bare `o portão se fecha` offer still has no antecedent and remains
an uncontested identity failure. See the
[follow-up result](IDENTITY-ASPECT-RESULT.md).

**Stage B met its local gate:** archived T36/T37/T38 and synthetic plain and
reopening sources all returned `retracted=false` in 4/4 calls; the synthetic
explicit correction returned `retracted=true` with the correction quoted in
4/4. The reader confirmed that reopening is a subsequent action, while the
correction says the first closing never occurred. Stage B's success does not
certify a closure happened, and it does not determine the final aperture after
reopening. Since Stage A failed, the combined candidate is stopped. No model
producer, private state comparison, proposal repair or renderer change follows.

The next unresolved boundary is target binding under ambiguous anaphora. A
future source-grounded candidate would need either enough offered context to
identify the physical gate without inventing certainty, or an explicit
`uncertain` outcome that prevents admission. That question needs a fresh
pre-registered test; the present screen does not justify a runtime prompt patch.
