# Hand-bound closed-event realizer: selected screen stopped

**Decision:** the frozen grammar/realizer pair fails the preregistered local
gate. This is a deterministic, hand-bound screen on four selected archived
beats, not evidence about a model producing semantic programs and not a
reliability estimate. No runtime path is admitted.

The [pre-registration](SEMANTIC-PROGRAM-FEASIBILITY-PREREGISTRATION.md)
fixed the four sources, source hashes, all-four rule and reader roles before
the output existed. The [script](semantic_program_feasibility.py) had SHA-256
`2e51f67cb524b28b1599ad7772cd16683eade981d9192da0559cc44159957529`
when it ran once. Its [exact output](semantic-program-feasibility-output.json)
has SHA-256
`20991f5ed30e50f3ec3db480c33aa4563e7276b4c82e6a82a90c1828847e1694`.
Source hashes matched at execution. The independent continuity and fiction
reader responses are [archived](semantic-program-feasibility-readers.json).

| Beat | Continuity reader, against supplied obligations | Fiction reader |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T38, sealed blue gate | Pass: no new closure or crossing | Fail: four repetitions of “azul” and two dry status statements make the attempt feel like a log |
| T8, legal opening | Pass: opening, wounded guard, commands, shield, smoke and bells retained | Fail: repeated “Garran”/“portões principais” and an abrupt list of one-clause actions; separately archived Character dialogue contains “Calei a boca” and conflicting orders for Link |
| T6, rubble | Pass: crossing, care, prayer, medical report, second tremor and rolling block retained without narrating Garran's postarrival action to origin viewers | Fail: four consecutive “Elowen” sentences, “bloco se solta dos blocos soltos”, and a narration of medical intent echoed by Character speech |
| T23, conversation | Pass: Noa's wait, distinct pressure, Maelis's tap and line condition retained | Fail: indirect descriptions pre-announce the Character dialogue; “de os alunos” is ungrammatical |

The readers **split by criterion**, not by vote: one factual reader accepted
four selected beats *against the obligations it received*, while one fiction
reader rejected all four as publishable RPG prose. These are four beat
judgements from each of two readers, not independent samples of a model error
rate.

There is a further limit to the factual audit. The accepted T6 Director
event says Garran **takes position beyond** the gap, while the reader's
origin-view packet intentionally says only that he crosses and disappears.
That omission respects the origin audience, but the screen does not provide
a second, far-side projection or demonstrate that the event is retained
elsewhere. Thus even the reader's four acceptances are **not** full semantic-program
coverage. The fixed event list also makes “Elowen considers...” a narrator
summary immediately before Elowen says it herself. The current project
contract gives final words to the Character agent; this hand-bound screen
appended archived words and did not test a new Character response.

The fiction reader's T8 complaints about “Calei a boca” and the two Link
orders refer to **archived Character words appended unchanged**, not words
generated by this realizer. They affect the assembled reader experience,
but cannot diagnose the realizer's own syntax. Likewise, Maelis's archived
“segure a entrada” differs from the Director's “feche a entrada”; the source
contract allows Character words to differ from Director intent. The
realizer's readability defect here is its redundant indirect command before
the final speech, not that the Character chose different words.

This negative result rejects **this frozen template-and-glossary pair** for
the registered local screen. It does not falsify every controlled realizer.
The next architecture question remains how to carry complete event semantics,
including offstage outcomes and speech intent, while allowing finished prose
without a second event authority. An unrestricted polishing call is only a
theoretical alternative here; it was not tested against the risk of adding an
untyped event.
