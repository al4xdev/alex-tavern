Here is the screenwriting and literary assessment of the four passages, evaluated against the source events, dramatic continuity, language quality in Brazilian Portuguese, and modular standalone viability.

### Passage P1

#### 1. Assembly & Dramatic Continuity (Fiction vs. Incident Reports)
P1 reads distinctly like **three decoupled incident reports** rather than continuous fiction. Each unit strictly follows an identical report template:
`[Full name introduction] -> [action beat] -> [atmospheric coda about dust/lights]`.
Because each unit resets its camera to zero and appends a decorative closing sentence about dust, reading them back-to-back creates a halting, repetitive loop.

#### 2. Textual Analysis & Exact Citations
* **Repeated Images / Stuttering:**
  * The dust motif is mechanically recycled at the end of every single unit:
    * Unit 1: *"...A poeira ainda flutua sob as lâmpadas de mana..."*
    * Unit 2: *"...A poeira assenta lentamente ao redor dos dois no chão claro."*
    * Unit 3: *"...envolto na poeira que se ergue e volta a assentar sobre as lâmpadas de mana."*
  * *Lâmpadas de mana* is repeated almost verbatim between Unit 1 (*"sob as lâmpadas de mana"*) and Unit 3 (*"sobre as lâmpadas de mana"*).
* **Inventory-like Restarts:**
  * Unit 1 opens with full title and name (*"A silhueta do instrutor Garran Holt..."*), Unit 2 completely ignores Garran’s crossing and re-anchors the scene from scratch with full names (*"Irmã Elowen Ramires cruza o piso poeirento e ajoelha-se junto a Cael Vesper"*), and Unit 3 performs another hard reset (*"Uma segunda vibração percorre o piso..."*).
* **Chronological Awkwardness:**
  * Unit 1 features a jarring narrative rewind: Garran disappears (*"some pelo vão estreito"*), the camera looks into the corridor on the other side (*"Do outro lado, o corredor lateral continua tomado..."*), and then suddenly snaps back to the entrance before he disappeared: *"...e sobre o piso rachado da entrada, a bota de Garran deixa uma marca funda antes de desaparecer na penumbra."* Staging this chronologically backwards disorients the reader.

#### 3. Standalone & Dangling Reference Audit
* **Unit 1:** Stands alone cleanly (identifies Garran Holt, the gap, and the obstructed corridor).
* **Unit 2:** Stands alone cleanly (identifies Elowen Ramires, Cael Vesper, the wound, and the prayer).
* **Unit 3:** Stands alone moderately well, though *"Uma segunda vibração"* slightly hints at an unshown initial shock.

#### 4. Source & Viewpoint
Respects the source facts without inventing post-crossing excursions, though the negative assertion *"sem que nenhuma voz retorne do interior escuro"* borders on editorializing.

**Verdict: PARTIAL**
*Grammatically sound and faithful to the source, but visually repetitive and structured like three separate dispatch notes rather than a cohesive dramatic sequence.*

### Passage P2

#### 1. Assembly & Dramatic Continuity (Fiction vs. Incident Reports)
P2 successfully reads as **continuous, engaging narrative prose**. Instead of feeling like isolated status cards, the transition between units mimics cinematic cross-cutting:
1. Tension and physical confinement with Garran at the breach;
2. Urgent, intimate physical triage with Elowen and Cael;
3. A sudden sensory escalation (seismic shock) that shatters the brief lull.

The prose flows with rhythmic cadence, tactile descriptions, and natural momentum.

#### 2. Textual Analysis & Exact Citations
* **Successes:**
  * *Tactile physical action:* *"os ombros raspando a pedra solta"* and *"a bota dele encontra piso firme e ele se endireita na penumbra"* capture the physical exertion of negotiating the breach.
  * *Sensory precision:* *"O tecido escurece depressa, e um fio de sangue contorna os dedos dela, escorrendo até o pulso."* Vivid, economical, and avoids medical-report detachment.
  * *Acoustic pacing:* *"a voz sai baixa, embalada pelo ritmo das palavras"* mirrors the steady murmur of stabilization rites.
  * *Environmental expansion:* In Unit 3, *"as lâmpadas de mana oscilam, lançando sombras inquietas pelas paredes do salão"* widens the frame effectively, showing the room's reaction to the tremor rather than just checking an inventory box.
* **Repeated Images / Friction:**
  * Dust appears in Unit 2 (*"enquanto a poeira ainda flutua em volta dos dois"*) and Unit 3 (*"A poeira levantada se adensa no ar"*). However, here it works as cause-and-effect: the dust settling around the triage is kicked up again by the falling block.

#### 3. Standalone & Dangling Reference Audit
* **Unit 1:** Fully standalone; identifies Garran Holt, the rubble gap, and the state of the forward corridor.
* **Unit 2:** Fully standalone; introduces Elowen Ramires and Cael Vesper with complete context.
* **Unit 3:** Fully standalone; the tremor, rockfall, and oscillating lamps are self-contained.

#### 4. Source & Viewpoint
Strictly observes the viewpoint of onlookers at the breach. Garran straightens up and looks ahead (*"O caminho à frente, porém, continua tomado..."*), assessing the blockage within origin sightlines without inventing actions deeper in the dark.

**Verdict: PASS**
*Rich sensory texture, natural dramatic transitions, solid standalone independence for each unit, and convincing Brazilian Portuguese prose rhythm.*

### Passage P3

#### 1. Assembly & Dramatic Continuity (Fiction vs. Incident Reports)
P3 sits midway between fiction and fragment. It has good atmosphere in places, but is severely held back by redundant prose phrasing, clumsy epithets, and a critical naming omission that breaks modularity.

#### 2. Textual Analysis & Exact Citations
* **Stylistic & Grammatical Awkwardness:**
  * *Redundant staging in Unit 2:* *"Irmã Elowen cruza o piso coberto de pó **até o jovem de joelhos**, dobra o corpo e **se ajoelha ao lado dele**."* Repeating "de joelhos" and "se ajoelha" within the same clause is clumsy draftsmanship.
  * *Epithet hunting in Unit 2:* *"Os lábios da **mulher de lenço verde** se movem numa prece murmurada..."* Referring to Elowen as "a mulher de lenço verde" immediately after naming her in the preceding sentence is unnatural.
  * *Ambiguous referent in Unit 2:* *"...comprimindo o tecido."* It is ambiguous whether "tecido" refers to the clean cloth, Cael's clothing, or body tissue.
* **Successes:**
  * Unit 3 opens smoothly: *"O chão volta a tremer, um solavanco curto que percorre as lajes claras."* Using *"volta a tremer"* avoids the mechanical counting of *"uma segunda vibração"*.

#### 3. Standalone & Dangling Reference Audit
* **Unit 1 FAILS the standalone test:** *"As botas **do instrutor** encontram as pedras soltas, e ele avança..."* **Garran Holt is never named.** If Unit 1 is served to a witness in isolation, "o instrutor" is an anonymous, dangling role.
* **Unit 2:** Semi-standalone, but awkward: Cael is introduced tangentially (*"até o jovem de joelhos... ombro ferido de Cael"*).
* **Unit 3:** Stands alone cleanly.

#### 4. Source & Viewpoint
Events match, but the perspective in Unit 1 is overly close to Garran's personal experience (*"assenta devagar sobre os ombros dele"*), straining what origin observers would clearly perceive.

**Verdict: PARTIAL**
*Contains decent sensory beats, but is disqualified from a PASS due to the un-named protagonist in Unit 1 (breaking standalone display) and redundant phrasing in Unit 2.*

### Passage P4

#### 1. Assembly & Dramatic Continuity (Fiction vs. Incident Reports)
P4 fails due to foreign language intrusion, character encoding corruption, spatial distortion of the core prompt, and overwritten melodrama.

#### 2. Textual Analysis & Exact Citations
* **Severe Language Glitch (Spanish Intrusion):**
  * Unit 2: *"...a voz baixa **corriendo** sob o ruído do salão."*
  * *"Corriendo"* is Spanish, not Portuguese (*correndo*). This breaks Brazilian Portuguese fluency immediately.
* **Encoding / Orthographic Defect:**
  * Unit 1: *"A pedra **raspа** no tecido do uniforme..."* Contains a non-Latin homoglyph (Cyrillic `а` / `U+0430`) in *"raspа"*.
* **Spatial Distortion of the Source:**
  * Unit 1 claims: *"...mas a passagem **atrás de si** continua atravancada por blocos soltos..."*
  * The source specifies that the corridor **beyond / ahead** remains partially obstructed, not the passage *behind* him. Inverting this reverses the tactical reality of the breach.
* **Invented Actions & Overwriting:**
  * Unit 1 invents micro-actions not witnessed or sourced: *"Ergue o queixo, planta as botas entre os detritos"*.
  * Unit 3 over-specifies decorative room geometry not in the source: *"faz tilintar as lâmpadas de mana **penduradas nos arcos**"*.

#### 3. Standalone & Dangling Reference Audit
* **Unit 1:** Opens with *"Não muito longe da fenda que rachou o piso"*, assuming prior knowledge of a floor fissure.
* **Unit 2:** Ruined by the Spanish verb form.
* **Unit 3:** Stands alone, but over-elaborated.

**Verdict: FAIL**
*Critical linguistic contamination ("corriendo"), spatial distortion of the corridor obstacle, encoding artifact ("raspа"), and unprompted narrative embellishments.*
