**Decision.** T32 remains the confirmed persisted **same-turn physical-state** contradiction in the source-complete portion of this selected read. T23 is excluded from the prose-fidelity comparison; it shows why the exact renderer request, including time-skip events, must be read before judging fidelity. Discarded-draft conflicts at T18, T26 and T34 show that a retry can remove an opposing statement; they do not show a net benefit rate. These observations neither establish that the correction caused T32 nor that retries generally reduce fidelity. The T32 real-payload retry comparison failed its all-eight-schema-valid prerequisite (7/8 valid), and the later fidelity-reader screen failed its all-twelve-schema-valid prerequisite (6/12 valid); neither scored its semantic decision gate. No prompt or runtime change follows from those screens.
