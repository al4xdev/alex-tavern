# T6 joint event units: local content gate failed

**Decision:** the frozen [one-call, three-unit prompt packet](T6-JOINT-UNITS-PREREGISTRATION.md)
is not admitted. Four direct DeepSeek curls returned distinct response IDs
and valid three-unit JSON with no replacement calls. The source-completeness
and fiction gates failed on the assembled texts. This is one archived T6
origin-view episode, not a provider failure rate or a runtime repair.

The frozen [manifest](t6-joint-units-manifest.json), [script](t6_joint_units_screen.py),
and [run directory](t6-joint-units-runs/) retain the exact requests, raw
envelopes, response IDs, durations and output units. The calls ran in
parallel; their individual durations were 3,239–3,478 ms. The reader packet
shuffled the four outputs as P1=run 3, P2=run 1, P3=run 4, P4=run 2. The
independent [continuity](t6-joint-units-continuity-reader.md) and
[fiction](t6-joint-units-fiction-reader.md) reports are preserved in full.

The continuity reader found the several-metre roll in all four texts, but
the stabilization purpose survived in only P1 and P4. The fiction reader
accepted P2 as prose while continuity rejected it on the omitted prayer.
This is disagreement by criterion, not a vote: fiction quality cannot excuse
a missing confirmed event. P3's unnamed first unit also defeats the
per-viewer separability requirement. The pre-registered **every-assembly
source gate** and **at-least-three fiction gate** both fail without needing
to resolve whether the lamp movement in P2 is harmless texture or a new
physical event.

This result rejects this exact joint-unit prompt packet on T6. It does
not show that independently generated units are better or worse, that a
different event-local contract is impossible, or that the post-move filter
can now be removed. The event-to-move note was still bound by hand; no
destination projection, incoming mover or offstage negative control ran.
The confirmed crossing remains vulnerable in the current runtime. The
next engineering boundary is event-to-move provenance: a safe post-move
guard needs to distinguish the crossing itself from later offstage action
without guessing from free-text names. This screen did not test that
boundary.
