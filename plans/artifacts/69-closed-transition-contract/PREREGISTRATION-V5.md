# Isolated continuity-selector agreement screen

Date: 2026-09-19. Fixed before the V5 calls are dispatched.

V4 produced four mechanically valid, zero-prose plans. A blind source reader,
before this screen was designed, accepted only V4-2 and vetoed V4-0, V4-1 and
V4-3 for causal, witness, simultaneity or pacing defects. V5 asks whether an
isolated production-model continuity judge can recover that independent verdict
without receiving the verdict or its justification.

## Variant

Keep the four archived V4 plans unchanged and relabel them opaquely in a fixed
order recorded only in the execution artifact. Give the judge:

- the same source user payload used by V4;
- the registered meanings of operations, cues and focus frames;
- the four opaque plans;
- criteria to veto settled-event repetition, causal-role mismatch, impossible
  witnesses, simultaneous actor overload, route/order contradiction, premature
  pacing, unsupported object use, dangling immediate consequence and absence of
  progress.

Do not include the prior reader's verdict, ranking, quotes or explanations. The
judge returns only enums: one selected plan or `NONE`, plus PASS/VETO and closed
reason codes for each plan. Four independent calls use the production
structured-output path, its existing attempt budget and an isolated temporary
data root.

The hidden mapping is:

- P = V4-1
- Q = V4-3
- R = V4-0
- S = V4-2

The independent reader's sole passing plan is therefore S. This mapping and
target are fixed before calls.

## Decision rule

The selector boundary is viable on this adjudication case only if:

- at least three of four judge calls are schema- and locally valid;
- at least three select S and mark S PASS;
- at least three mark each of P, Q and R VETO;
- no call selects P, Q or R;
- selected-S calls cite no reason code against S.

Failure rejects same-model semantic selection on this case. Passing establishes
agreement on one already-read batch only. It permits a fresh candidate batch
with a newly blind external read; it does not validate a renderer or runtime
implementation.
