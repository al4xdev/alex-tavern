Source comparison narrows the repeat finding. T17's accepted Director event
opens a west-wall breach and releases a swarm; its `scene_update` commits
`terceira_brecha: aberta na parede oeste` and `enxame_fragmentos: ativo`.
T18's Director request **still** carries the same `Current beat: Uma terceira
brecha se abre ... liberando um enxame ...`. **Correction on re-reading the
archived request:** its `Not in play yet` list **still contains** `terceira
brecha`; the earlier sentence said the opposite. The accepted T18 event then says
`uma terceira brecha se abre` anew, and the saved narration presents it as
`Uma terceira brecha se escancara`. T19 retains the same beat intent and its
event/narration has the swarm emerge from the third breach again. As written,
T18 presents an opening after T17's committed opening without identifying
a different hole or saying the first widened. Widening is a possible reading
of `se escancara`, so the exact aperture transition remains ambiguous. This
is an input/output juxtaposition, **not** proof that the unchanged beat intent
caused the repeat; earlier wording experiments on other payloads did not
validate a correction.

The reader also saw the shield impact on Seraphine repeated almost verbatim
in T17 and T18. Both accepted Director events contain the impact, so it is
not a renderer-only echo. A second impact is physically possible, but no
distinct cause or consequence is supplied; keep it as a likely restaging
for this read, not a proved duplicate transition. Doran's rune work and
Bruna's trap preparation recur, yet the source does not pin one unique
workpiece or route from east to west. Their continuity remains ambiguous.
Riven's repeated shoulder hits may be separate blows with accumulating
damage; the reader explicitly treated them as such. T20's larger wall
collapse and golem emergence is an escalation, not another instance of
the original breach opening.
