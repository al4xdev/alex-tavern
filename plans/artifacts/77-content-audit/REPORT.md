# Task 77: three cited windows read as fiction (2026-09-24)

Scope: the three windows named in the task, not a random sample or an estimate of defect frequency. Source is the persisted visible history and accepted Director responses in `plans/artifacts/repetition-battery/base-P1-r1/sessions/09aabf25/` and `base-P1-r2/sessions/b11b38dc/`. Three independent scout calls each saw only one projected text excerpt, with no code, metrics or task. Their literary judgments prompted source checks; they are not a calibrated judge, acceptance score or proof of model intent. No prompt variant or new model-behavior claim is tested here.

1. `09aabf25` T30–T35 is a real **unresolved crossing under repeated orders**, not an immobile scene. At T30 the narration says Asword advances toward the north gate supported by Link; T31's shock wave pushes nearby students back; T32 they advance again; T34 the pair advances through an opened passage; T35 they are still crossing a makeshift bridge over the fissure. Maelis nonetheless repeats immediate-entry orders at T30–T35. A text-only reader found the orders dramatically redundant because they do not adapt to the changing obstacles. That is a literary judgment, not proof that the engine could or should have completed the crossing sooner. The text does not clearly say the pair entered the dungeon by T35; it also does not warrant “nobody moves.” T33 and T34 both narrate a “second howl” and the fissure opening in the same place, in both persisted narration and accepted Director events. This reads as an event re-proposal overlapping task 69, without proving it is the same event or what caused it.

2. `b11b38dc` T20–T24 is **not an unambiguous corroborating instance of nobody executing an order**. T20 explicitly says Riven crosses the threshold and disappears down the corridor, and the Director's top-level `zone_moves` records C13 entering `corredor da masmorra`. Link says “Não se preocupem comigo, continuem” at T21 and remains near the table; fog holds Asword back while the opening narrows; T24 closes the door and leaves Asword outside. A text-only reader interpreted the reiterated orders as motivated by refusal and increasing physical obstruction. That interpretation does not prove the model repeated them deliberately for drama. T22 and T23 have no recorded zone move, but the doorway narrows and Maelis steps toward Asword. No movement count alone can classify those repeated orders as a defect.

3. `09aabf25` T15–T17 is **ambiguous crossing followed by apparent reset**, not simply nobody entering. T15 narration says groups of students “precipitam-se para dentro do corredor escuro”; T16 has groups of students moving toward the gate; T17 has students gathered by it. Because the groups are unnamed, the text does not prove they are identical. It also never distinguishes an advance group from a remaining group, leaving the reader unable to track who has crossed. A fresh text-only reader called out the same ambiguity and also noticed Link supporting Asword afresh over these turns. T15 top-level `zone_moves` moves two named characters to courtyard zones, not to the corridor; T16–T17 have no zone moves. Those fields do not identify which anonymous students, if any, crossed into the corridor.

Consequences for the task's evidence: the headline “nobody moves” and the claim that all three windows show the same defect overstate the text. One window supports repeated commands without a clearly completed gate crossing; one is a failed evacuation with a legible obstacle and a closed door; one is an unclear collective crossing/reset. The historical 13/33-session, 23-window report counted restated lines plus unchanged recorded positions. Its historical control, recorded in the task, found restated-order pairs on frozen positions in 36/39 cases versus 184/213 adjacent pairs overall (pooled Fisher p=0.43); that did not discriminate stalls from ordinary pairs. This control is itself a pooled positional proxy, not a literary quality test. These selected reads find at least one ambiguous or plausibly justified repeated order. In the main case Link and Asword advance within the hall while their persisted position stays `salão, próximo ao portão norte` from T29 through T35; T35's `zone_moves` repeats that same zone. Thus 13/33 is **an archived detector-incidence report**, not validated defect prevalence. This audit does not classify the other windows and supplies no precision, recall or replacement rate. Task 77 remains open as a content problem with unknown cause; task 72 should not inherit a causal claim from these windows. Research can continue now by reading additional cases with the same separation of narrated movement, actual input and committed state. A post-69 comparison would test whether this symptom survives any eventual producer change; it is not a prerequisite for further reading.
